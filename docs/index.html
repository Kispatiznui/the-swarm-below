THE DESCENT

SOMETHING IS MOVING BELOW.

THE SWARM BELOW is a dark atmospheric survival-action game developed by Kispat Dreams Interactive.

You take control of the Witness, an entity trapped within an ever-growing swarm of aberrations.

Move. Fight. Gather Echo. Evolve. Survive the swarm.

SURVIVAL PROTOCOL

HOW TO PLAY

01

MOVE

Navigate the battlefield and avoid the approaching swarm.

02

FIGHT

Use the Witness's defensive abilities to destroy the aberrations.

03

COLLECT

Gather Echo and Memory while surviving increasingly dangerous waves.

04

EVOLVE

Spend Echo on upgrades and become stronger as the swarm grows.

SURVIVAL SYSTEMS

INTEGRITY

Your physical condition. Lose all Integrity and the run ends.

ENERGY

Powers your defensive abilities. Manage it carefully.

ECHO

The primary resource used to purchase upgrades.

MEMORY

A rarer resource connected to the deeper systems of the game.

COMBO

Maintain your momentum by destroying enemies continuously.

WAVES

Each wave pushes the swarm further and further.

ADAPTATION

EVOLVE OR BE CONSUMED.

Every successful progression opens a new opportunity to strengthen the Witness.

Choose from randomized upgrades, spend Echo and adapt your build to the swarm.

The stronger you become, the more the swarm demands from you.
