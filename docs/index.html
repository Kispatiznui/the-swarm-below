THE DESCENT

SOMETHING IS MOVING BELOW.

THE SWARM BELOW is a dark atmospheric survival-action game developed by Kispat Dreams Interactive.

You take control of the Witness, an entity trapped within an ever-growing swarm of aberrations.

Move. Fight. Gather Echo. Evolve. Survive the swarm.

SURVIVAL DOCTRINE

FIELD NOTES

WHEN THEY SURROUND YOU

THORN clears space around you. Use it before a cluster finishes forming, not after.

WHEN A CONVERGENCE FORMS

CHORUS is your answer for a real crowd. Its cooldown is long — save it, don't spend it on stragglers.

WHEN YOU NEED TIME

GRAVITY WOMB doesn't kill anything. It buys you room to think, without spending your bigger tools.

WHEN THEY'RE ALREADY WOUNDED

THE MOUTH finishes weakened fragments in melee range and feeds your energy back — high risk, real reward.

WHEN THE FIELD IS LITTERED WITH ECHO

THE CALL pulls it all to you at once. It also tells the entire swarm exactly where you are. Use it when the field is under control, not before.

WHEN YOU NEED TO LEAVE

DASH repositions you fast — but grants no immunity. It moves you out of danger. It does not walk you through it.
